{% trans "How it works" %}

{% trans "Two simple paths — whether you need a book or you're done with one." %}

{% trans "If you need a book" %}
  1. {% blocktrans trimmed %}Search or browse by title, author, ISBN, subject, board or university. Save a search or post a request and we'll tell you when it's listed.{% endblocktrans %}
  2. {% blocktrans trimmed %}Chat, offer, and buy or rent — ask the seller questions, make an offer, then pay by UPI, card, wallet or cash on delivery.{% endblocktrans %}
  3. {% blocktrans trimmed %}Track it and confirm — follow the courier, then enter the delivery OTP to confirm the book is in your hands.{% endblocktrans %}
{% trans "If you have books to pass on" %}
  1. {% blocktrans trimmed %}List it in minutes — scan the cover, get an AI-suggested price, and let duplicate checks keep the catalogue clean.{% endblocktrans %}
  2. {% blocktrans trimmed %}Ship when it sells — accept the order and hand it to the courier; digital notes are delivered automatically.{% endblocktrans %}
  3. {% blocktrans trimmed %}Get paid by UPI — your payout is released once the buyer confirms delivery, and you can download a commission statement any time.{% endblocktrans %}
